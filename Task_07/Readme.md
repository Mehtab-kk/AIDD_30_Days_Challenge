## 5 core concept (short answer)

- **constitution**  
  The constitution defines the fundamental principles and rules of a system or organization. It acts as a guide for how things should function.

- **specify**  
  To specify means to clearly state or describe something in detail, leaving no confusion.

- **plan**  
  Planning involves outlining steps or actions needed to achieve a goal effectively.

- **tasks**  
  Tasks are specific pieces of work or activities assigned to complete a plan or objective.
